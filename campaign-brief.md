---

## Universal creative rules

- **Aspect ratios per ad:** 1080×1080 (1:1 feed) AND 1080×1920 (9:16 Stories/Reels)
- **Background:** Dark navy `#072B4A` (or `#051D33`). Never light backgrounds.
- **Accent:** Gold `#F5C518`. Used sparingly — headline accent words, single CTA, thin border lines.
- **Typography:**
  - Headlines: **Barlow Condensed, weight 800**, tight tracking, near-zero line-height
  - Eyebrows (small label above headline): Poppins 700, all-caps, tracking widest, gold colour
  - Body: Poppins 400-500
- **Logo:** PT Launch Lab logo top-left, max 80px height in 1080×1920 frame
- **Safe zone:** keep important text within middle 80% of frame (avoid edges + Stories UI overlap zones at top/bottom)
- **Text overlay rule:** text should occupy 30-50% of frame — not 80% (too text-heavy), not 10% (no hook)
- **Do NOT use:** stock fitness imagery, neon scarcity badges, fake "expires soon" overlays, gradient rainbow effects, light backgrounds, serif fonts
