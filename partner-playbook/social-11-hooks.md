The first line decides whether the rest gets read. Take any of these, add your own second line, post it.

**Aimed at people who've never considered it**
```
Some of you already coach. You just don't get paid for it.
You're in here five days a week. Ever thought about being paid to be?
The person everyone asks for advice is usually the last to consider doing it properly.
You've spent years learning this. That's worth something.
Half of you could do this job. Most of you have never been told.
```

**Career dissatisfaction**
```
Sunday night feeling? There's a version of Monday you'd look forward to.
You don't hate working. You hate that job.
What if the gym wasn't the bit of your day you had to squeeze in?
Nobody grows up dreaming of a desk.
The best part of your day is 45 minutes long. It doesn't have to be.
```

**Objection-led**
```
You don't need abs to be a good coach.
You're not too old. Most people who start with us are 30 to 45.
"I haven't got time" is why it's built around a job.
You don't need to be the strongest person in the room. You need to be the most patient.
No, you don't have to quit your job first.
```

**Curiosity**
```
Can we ask you something?
Nobody talks about how PTs actually got started.
Here's what our coaches did before this.
There's a question we ask members that catches them off guard.
Three people in this gym are training to become PTs. You'd never guess which.
```

**Proof**
```
[Name] was a [job] two years ago.
This is what a career change actually looks like.
Everyone on this gym floor started as somebody's member.
Nobody starts qualified.
```

**Direct**
```
Applications close [DATE].
We're running an academy in this gym. Here's what that means for you.
Two spaces left on this intake.
If you've been thinking about it, this is the bit where you ask.
```

**How to use them:** pick a hook, add one line of context, add one line of ask. Three lines total. The instinct to explain everything in the caption is what kills reach — the explaining happens in the DM.

**Rotate them.** Using the same hook twice in a month teaches people to scroll past your posts, which costs you more than one weak post ever would.
