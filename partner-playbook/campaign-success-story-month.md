title: Success story month
type: campaign
channel: Any time
when_to_use: Once you have anyone enrolled. Nothing converts like proof.

For one month, post nothing about the academy except real people. No graphics, no offers, no explaining how it works.

What counts as a story:

- Someone who's just started
- A career change: what they did before
- Finishing a unit, not just the whole course
- Qualifying
- First client
- First day coaching

**You don't need a graduate.** Someone six weeks in is a story, and it's the one your members can actually picture themselves in. See *Proof, before you have any graduates*.

## Two rules

**Ask properly.** Their name, their photo and the fact they're studying are theirs, not yours. Show them the post first and make it easy to say no.

**Never post what anyone earns.** Even if they offer, even if it's impressive. An income figure in a marketing post implies a typical result nobody can stand behind, and it's the fastest way to a complaint you can't defend.

People buy stories, not qualifications. But only true ones survive contact with the member who signs up expecting the same.
