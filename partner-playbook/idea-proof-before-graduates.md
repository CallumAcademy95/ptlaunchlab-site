---
title: Proof, before you have any graduates
type: idea
channel: Read this first
when_to_use: You have just launched and feel like you have nothing to show
order: 5
---

Every gym starting this says the same thing: *"We'll push it properly once someone's qualified."*

That's twelve months of doing nothing, and it isn't necessary. You already have three kinds of proof.

## 1. Anyone who has enrolled

Someone who **started** is better proof than someone who finished, because starting is the bit your members can't picture themselves doing. Finishing is abstract. Starting is a decision they could make on Thursday.

*"[Name] joined here two years ago, works full-time, and is now training to qualify"* does almost everything a graduate story does, and you have it the week your first member enrols.

## 2. Your existing coaches

Every trainer on your floor came from somewhere. Ask them what they did before, and you'll get retail, construction, teaching, hospitality, an office.

That's the story your members need, and it's been standing in your gym the whole time. Nobody's ever asked them.

## 3. The number

If you've had anyone enrol at all, you can say so without naming anyone and without asking anyone's permission:

> *"Right now, three of our members are training to become qualified Personal Trainers."*

Small numbers work better than you'd think. Three is believable in a way three hundred isn't, and it makes the point that this is a real thing happening here rather than an advert.

## What not to do

**Don't borrow someone else's numbers.** Not another gym's learners, not ours. Your members can tell the difference between *"people do this"* and *"people here do this"*, and only the second one moves them.

**Don't wait for a perfect story.** The first one is never the best one. Run the ordinary version now and upgrade it in six months when somebody qualifies, by which point you'll have had six months of conversations you'd otherwise have skipped.
