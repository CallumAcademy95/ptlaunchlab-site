---
title: The follow-up nobody sends
type: email
channel: Enquirers
when_to_use: Two to three weeks after someone showed interest and stopped replying
order: 60
---

Most enquiries die here. Not because the person changed their mind, but because nobody followed up and they assumed that meant it wasn't for them.

```
Subject: Still thinking about becoming a PT?

Hi [First Name],

A little while back you asked about becoming a Personal Trainer. Just checking
in.

If you've still got questions, or you're not sure whether now's the right time,
we're happy to help either way.

Sometimes one conversation makes the next step a lot clearer.

Reply to this and we'll point you in the right direction.

The [Gym Name] Team
```

Send it once. If there's no reply, leave it — they know where you are, and a third email turns a warm person cold.
