Phone, no script, under 30 seconds. Filmed badly beats not filmed.

**Ask your trainer.** Point a camera at a coach and ask one question
```
What did you do before this?
What's the worst advice you got starting out?
What do you wish you'd known?
Hardest part of the job?
Best part nobody talks about?
What surprised you most?
Do you still get nervous?
What's your first client story?
Would you go back to your old job?
What would you tell yourself three years ago?
```

**Myths.** One myth, one correction, done
```
You need to look a certain way
It's all shouting and counting reps
You have to quit your job first
You need years of experience
It's only for young people
You have to be a "gym person"
Everyone earns the same
You need your own gym
It's all early mornings
You can't do it around kids
```

**Day in the life.** 6 to 8 clips of a real day, no narration
```
First client of the day
What a session actually looks like
The admin nobody shows
Lunch between clients
Programme writing
Talking to a member on the floor
End of day
What the job looks like at 6am / at 8pm
```

**Career change.** The strongest category
```
What I did before
Why I changed
What my family said
The moment I decided
First month reality
What I'd tell someone hesitating
How long it actually took
What I got wrong
```

**Explainer.** Answer one question in 20 seconds
```
How long does it take?
Can I do it around a job?
What qualification is it?
Do I need experience?
What happens after I qualify?
How much study a week?
Is it online or in person?
What's the first step?
```

**Gym-led**
```
Why we started an academy here
What we look for in someone who'd be good at this
A member who's started, in their words
Three of our members are training right now
```

## Rules that make these work

**Under 30 seconds.** The answer to "hardest part of the job" is 15 seconds long. Anything more is a podcast.

**No intro.** Start on the answer. "Hi guys, so today I wanted to talk about…" loses half the viewers before the content starts.

**Subtitles always.** Most of this gets watched on mute in a changing room.

**Do it in one sitting.** Film ten answers with a coach in twenty minutes and you have ten weeks of content. Trying to film one a week means you'll film two.
