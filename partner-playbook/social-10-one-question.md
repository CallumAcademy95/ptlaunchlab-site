**Image:** plain text on your brand colour. Deliberately unproduced.

```
Can we ask you something?

Have you ever thought about doing this professionally?

If the answer is "maybe" — come and have a chat.

That one conversation could change your future.
```

The shortest post in the library and often the best performing. It asks one question, offers no information, and the only way to satisfy the curiosity is to reply.
