title: "How much does it cost?"
type: script
channel: In gym
when_to_use: Any time price comes up, and it always does
order: 40
---

Never avoid it. Never go awkward. Awkwardness about price tells someone there's something wrong with the price.

```
It depends which payment option you go for — there are flexible plans, so most
people spread it.

Rather than me guessing, PT Launch Lab will walk you through the options
properly and answer anything you've got.
```

If you know the current public pricing and payment plans, **it is fine to state them accurately.** What is not fine is inventing a figure, promising a discount, or quoting a price that isn't live.

The rule: be exact, or hand it over. Never approximate.
