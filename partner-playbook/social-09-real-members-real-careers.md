---
title: Real members, real careers
type: social
channel: Instagram + Facebook
when_to_use: As soon as one member has enrolled. You don't need a graduate.
order: 90
---

The mistake is waiting for a success story. You don't need someone who's finished — you need someone who **started**, because starting is the leap your other members can't picture themselves making.

**Image:** your enrolled member training, or helping someone. Ask first.

```
The best Personal Trainers don't start as Personal Trainers.

They start as members.

[NAME] joined here [WHEN]. Trains after work, like everyone else.

They're now training to become a qualified PT — around a full-time job.

Same gym. Same start. They just decided to find out.

Maybe the next one is you.
```

**If nobody has agreed to be named yet**, this version needs no permission and is just as true:

```
Right now, [NUMBER] of our members are training to become qualified Personal
Trainers.

Same gym as you. Same start as you.

They just decided to find out what was possible.
```

**Get permission properly.** A member's name, photo and the fact they're studying is their information, not yours. Ask, show them the post before it goes out, and make it easy to say no.

**Upgrade it when someone qualifies.** Swap "training to become" for "is now a qualified PT, here", add a line in their own words about why they did it, and it becomes the strongest post you'll ever run. Never add what they earn, even if they offer.
