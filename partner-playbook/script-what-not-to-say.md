---
title: What not to say
type: script
channel: Team briefing
when_to_use: Brief every member of staff on this before they speak to anyone
order: 70
---

The most important page in this playbook. Never promise something you don't control. It exposes your gym, it exposes us, and it lets down the person who believed it.

| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "You'll definitely get a job." | "We'll help you explore opportunities once you qualify." |
| "You'll earn loads of money." | "Plenty build rewarding careers, but income depends on your clients, your hours and the route you take." |
| "This course is easy." | "It's built to be manageable alongside work, but it does take commitment." |
| "You'll qualify in exactly eight weeks." | "Most people finish in the expected timeframe, depending how consistently they study." |
| "Everyone passes." | "You're supported the whole way through, to give you the best chance." |
| "You should definitely do it." | "If it's something you've been thinking about, it's worth a conversation." |

**Never advise anyone on finance.** Not on affordability, not on borrowing, not on whether they can manage the payments. Hand that to the academy team every time, without exception.
