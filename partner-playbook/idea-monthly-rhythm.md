You don't need to market the academy every day. You need a few focused things, repeated.

## The four-week loop

| Week | Focus | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Awareness | One post, a couple of stories |
| 2 | Conversations | Staff challenge: three each |
| 3 | Proof | A member or coach story |
| 4 | Ask | Intake reminder + email |

Then start again. Simple beats clever here, because clever doesn't survive a busy month.

## Six questions at the start of each month

- Have we posted about the academy?
- Have we sent an email?
- Are the posters still up, and not behind something?
- Do the QR codes still scan?
- Have we celebrated a learner or a coach?
- Has every staff member had at least one conversation?

Six yeses means you're doing the job. You don't need anything else.

## Ten minutes in the team meeting

- Who have we spoken to?
- What did people ask that we couldn't answer?
- Which post did best?
- Any new stories?
- Who are we speaking to next month?

Question two is the valuable one. The questions your team can't answer are the questions your members have. Pass them to the academy team and the answer comes back for everyone.
