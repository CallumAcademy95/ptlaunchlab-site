Your job isn't to sell the course. It's to help someone notice an opportunity they've never seriously considered.

Every conversation is the same four steps.

1. **Notice** — "I've noticed you're always helping people around the gym."
2. **Ask** — "Have you ever thought about doing this professionally?"
3. **Listen** — let them talk. Don't fill the silence.
4. **Guide** — point them at the QR code, or offer to put them in touch.

The person asking the questions is usually the person most interested. The moment you're doing most of the talking, you've stopped helping and started pitching.

**Three rules for the whole team**

- **Be curious.** Don't decide in advance who isn't interested. You'll be wrong.
- **Be honest.** "I'm not sure, but PT Launch Lab can explain that properly" is a complete answer.
- **Leave them inspired.** Nobody should walk away feeling sold to. They should walk away thinking *maybe I could actually do this.*
