title: "Will I actually get work afterwards?"
type: script
channel: In gym
when_to_use: The real question underneath all the others

---

This is the one that decides it. Members are buying the belief that it leads somewhere, not the qualification itself.

Which is exactly why you must not oversell it.

```
The aim is to help people build a career, not just pass an exam.

Graduates go on to work in gyms, go self-employed, or combine PT with another
role. Every route looks a bit different.

I'd rather be straight with you about what's realistic than promise something
nobody can guarantee.
```

No guarantee, no income figure, no "we'll hire you." Honesty converts better here than enthusiasm, because the person asking has been sold to before and didn't believe it.
