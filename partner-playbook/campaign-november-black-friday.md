There is no Black Friday price. The course costs the same every day of the year: £999.99 in full, or 10 monthly payments of £99.99 with no interest. No codes, no sale week.

That is on purpose. A price that drops once a year teaches members to wait for it, and makes everyone who enrolled in October feel they overpaid.

## If a member asks

```
No Black Friday deal, I'm afraid. The price is the same all year,
so nobody misses out by starting now. You can pay monthly if that helps.
Want me to send you the link?
```

## What to run in November instead

Run any campaign from **The year ahead** that you haven't used yet. November is a good month to answer the money question without a sale: say plainly that it's 10 monthly payments, with no interest, and that members can start any day.

Don't post "no Black Friday deal" unprompted. Just answer the question when it comes.
