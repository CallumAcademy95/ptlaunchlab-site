## The DM reply script

Reply speed is the whole campaign, and this is where it's won or lost — reply within the hour if you can. A Black Friday DM left until tomorrow is a lost sale.

Fill three baskets before you recommend anything, same as any other conversation:

1. **Present situation.** *"What's got you asking now?"* — let them answer in full.
2. **The prize.** *"What would qualifying actually get you?"* — their answer, not yours.
3. **The problem.** *"What's stopped you before?"* — usually time or money. This week answers the money one.

Only once you've heard all three, send this:

```
It's £999 this week if you pay in full — normally £1,399. Code {{bfCode}} at {{academyUrl}}. Covers Level 2 and Level 3 together, plus the mentorship community. Price goes back up Monday. Want me to send the link?
```

## What to judge it on

Enrolments. **This is the one month that is fair to judge on sales** — the offer is unambiguous, the window is short, and the code tells you exactly which ones came from you.
