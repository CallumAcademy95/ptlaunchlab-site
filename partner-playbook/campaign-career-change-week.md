Aimed squarely at people who are fed up at work and have never connected that to the gym they're standing in.

**The line:** *Love fitness. Hate your job?*

## The week

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| **Monday** | *"What's your dream job?"* — a question, nothing else |
| **Tuesday** | A member's story |
| **Wednesday** | Coach spotlight — what they did before |
| **Thursday** | FAQ video. Film it on a phone, answer the four questions you always get |
| **Friday** | Applications open |

## The staff challenge

Every member of the team asks five people the same question that week:

```
If money wasn't an issue, what would you actually do for work?
```

It's a good conversation regardless. If fitness comes up, you're already talking about it and nobody has been sold anything.

Keep a tally on the staff noticeboard — conversations had, not enrolments. What gets counted gets done, and enrolments are too far downstream to motivate anyone in week one.
