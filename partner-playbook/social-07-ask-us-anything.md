title: Ask us anything
type: social
channel: Instagram Story
when_to_use: Once a month. This is the post that generates conversations.
order: 70
---

Use the **question sticker**, not a caption.

```
Thinking about becoming a Personal Trainer but not sure where to start?

Drop a question below, or send us a DM.

There are no silly questions. If you've been wondering — ask.
```

Then answer every single one publicly on your story, including the awkward ones about money. Ten people are reading each answer for every one who asked.

The names that come in here go straight to your staff. Someone who asked a question this week is a conversation on the gym floor next week.
