---
title: "I haven't got the time"
type: script
channel: In gym
when_to_use: The most common objection you will hear
order: 50
---

Never argue with it. Never try to "overcome" it. They're right, and they are busy.

```
Completely understand. Most people who enrol are already working full-time.

That's actually why it's built the way it is, designed to fit around a job
rather than replace one.
```

Pause. Then:

```
Would it help if I showed you how it works?
```

Notice what you haven't done: told them they're wrong, told them to make time, or listed benefits. You agreed with them and offered information. That's the whole move.
