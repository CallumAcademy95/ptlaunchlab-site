---
title: A second nudge to enquirers
type: email
channel: Enquirers
when_to_use: A week or two after someone enquired and went quiet
order: 50
---

To people who have already raised a hand, never the whole list. Sent to everyone it's an advert. Sent to someone who enquired, it's a reminder.

```
Subject: Still thinking about it?

Hi [First Name],

You asked about our academy a little while ago. You can start any day,
so there's no rush, but if becoming a Personal Trainer is still on your
mind, now is a good moment to ask the questions you've been putting off.

No pressure. No obligation. Just a conversation about whether it's right for
you.

The [Gym Name] Team
```

Never invent a deadline. Enrolment is rolling, so there is no intake to close, and a made-up date teaches people to ignore everything you send after it.
