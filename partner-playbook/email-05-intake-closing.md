---
title: Intake closing
type: email
channel: Enquirers
when_to_use: Five to seven days before an intake closes
order: 50
---

To people who have already raised a hand, never the whole list. A deadline sent to everyone is an advert. Sent to someone who enquired, it's a reminder.

```
Subject: Don't miss this one

Hi [First Name],

Applications for our next academy intake close on [DATE].

If becoming a Personal Trainer has ever crossed your mind, this is a good
moment to ask the questions you've been putting off.

No pressure. No obligation. Just a conversation about whether it's right for
you.

The [Gym Name] Team
```

Put the real date in. If it moves, tell them it moved — a deadline you quietly extend teaches people to ignore the next one.
