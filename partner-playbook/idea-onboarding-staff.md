---
title: Bringing new staff up to speed
type: idea
channel: Getting started
when_to_use: Every new starter, from now on
order: 25
---

Five minutes in every induction. The academy should be part of how you onboard, not something people pick up eventually.

Cover six things:

1. **What the academy is.** We help members qualify as Personal Trainers, here.
2. **Why we run it.** Members who want a career in fitness shouldn't have to go elsewhere for it.
3. **Who's a good fit.** The ones who help everyone else, the ones people ask for advice.
4. **How to start the conversation.** The four-step framework, and the poster script.
5. **Where the QR codes are.** Walk them round and point at them.
6. **Who to ask.** The one person on your team who handles questions nobody can answer.

Then hand them *What not to say*. It's the only part they need to get right first time, because everything else is recoverable and that isn't.

**Why five minutes matters:** a new starter who's never been told about the academy will confidently tell a member it isn't a thing here. That's happened at gyms with posters on the wall.
