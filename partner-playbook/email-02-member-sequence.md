---
title: The six-email member sequence
type: email
channel: Whole list
when_to_use: Over six weeks, one a week, after the launch announcement
order: 20
---

Six emails, one a week. They build — a question, then recognition, then dissatisfaction, then permission, then the picture, then the nudge. Sent as a set they do far more than any one of them alone.

**1 — The question**
```
Subject: Can I ask you something?

Hi {{First Name}},

Can I ask you a genuine question?

Have you ever thought about working in fitness?

Not being an influencer. Not competing. Not living in the gym.

Just helping people get healthier, every day.

I ask because we now run our own Personal Training academy, here in the gym.

The funny thing is, most people who end up brilliant at this never planned on
it. They were members who loved training.

If it's ever crossed your mind — even for a second — we'd love a chat.

No pressure. No sales pitch. Just a conversation.

See you in the gym,
The {{Gym Name}} Team

P.S. Next time you're in, ask us about the academy. You might be surprised
what's possible.
```

**2 — You probably don't realise it**
```
Subject: We've noticed something about you...

Hi {{First Name}},

Every gym has certain members.

The ones who encourage other people. The ones everyone asks for advice. The
ones who genuinely enjoy helping.

They're usually the people who end up being brilliant Personal Trainers.

The qualification teaches the technical side. But patience, kindness,
encouragement? You've either got those or you haven't.

If you've ever wondered whether coaching might be for you, we'd like to point
you in the right direction.

Reply to this, or grab one of the team next time you're in.
```

**3 — What if Monday didn't feel like Monday**
```
Subject: Do you enjoy what you do?

Hi {{First Name}},

Here's a question most people avoid.

Do you actually enjoy your job?

For a lot of people the honest answer is "not really".

That's why so many career changes start in a gym. People notice they're
happiest when they're training, learning, helping.

They don't fall in love with a qualification. They just decide they want a
different future.

If you've ever thought "I'd love to work in fitness" — maybe it's worth finding
out what that would actually look like.

No commitment. Just honest advice.
```

**4 — Every coach started somewhere**
```
Subject: Every Personal Trainer has one thing in common

Hi {{First Name}},

Every trainer on our gym floor has one thing in common.

At some point, they were standing exactly where you are.

They weren't experts. They didn't know everything. They just decided to learn.

Nobody is born knowing how to coach.

If you've ever pictured yourself helping people reach their goals, we'd like to
show you what that looks like.

The first step isn't enrolling. It's a conversation. That's it.
```

**5 — Imagine this**
```
Subject: Imagine this is your job

Hi {{First Name}},

Imagine getting to the gym on a Monday morning.

Not to train. To work.

You spend the day helping people feel stronger, healthier, more confident. You
celebrate PBs. You watch people change.

You go home knowing you actually made a difference to someone.

That's why people choose this career. Not because it's easy — because it
matters.

If you've ever wondered whether you could do something like this, let's have a
chat. You might be closer than you think.
```

**6 — Don't leave it as a what if**
```
Subject: One last thing...

Hi {{First Name}},

Every year we speak to members who say the same thing.

"I've been thinking about becoming a PT for ages."

The problem is that thinking doesn't change anything. Conversations do.

If working in fitness has ever crossed your mind, don't leave it as another
what if.

Come and speak to us. Ask your questions. Find out what's involved.

If it's right for you, brilliant. If it isn't, that's completely fine too.

Either way, you'll know.

See you soon,
The {{Gym Name}} Team
```

Don't compress this into a fortnight. A week between emails is what makes it feel like someone talking rather than a campaign running.
