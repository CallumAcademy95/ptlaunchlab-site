---
title: The coach spotting challenge
type: campaign
channel: Team-led
when_to_use: At your next staff meeting. Costs nothing, takes ten minutes.
order: 70
---

Give every member of the team the same task:

> Write down five members who'd make a brilliant Personal Trainer.

**Everyone joins in.** Reception, coaches, cleaners, managers. The person who mops the floor at 6am sees a completely different set of members to the PT who works evenings, and they see them without an agenda.

## At the end of the week

Compare the lists. **The names that appear more than once are your highest-potential learners**, not because the team is right about who wants it, but because several people independently noticed the same thing about them.

Start with those conversations. Use the gym floor script.

## Why this works better than a poster

A poster waits for someone to already be thinking about it. This finds the people who'd be brilliant and have never once considered it, which is most of them.

It also does something to your team. Once someone has written five names down, they start noticing a sixth. That's a lasting change in how they look at the gym, from one ten-minute exercise.
