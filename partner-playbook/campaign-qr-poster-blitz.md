The easiest thing on this list and the one most often done badly.

## Where they go

Reception desk · entrance doors · changing rooms · PT consultation room · the studio · café or seating · noticeboard · membership packs · your welcome email · your social profiles.

**The best spots are where people stand still and have nothing to do.** The changing room mirror, the wall you face on the leg press, the queue at reception. A poster in a corridor is a poster nobody reads.

## Never print "Scan me"

It tells someone what to do without telling them why. Use:

```
Thinking about becoming a Personal Trainer?

Scan here to find out more.
```

The question qualifies the scan. Fewer people scan, more of them mean it.

## Check them monthly

QR codes get covered by other posters, printed too small, faded by the window, or stuck somewhere with no phone signal. **Scan every one yourself once a month.** A dead QR code looks identical to a working one, and you'd never know until someone mentioned it, which they won't.
