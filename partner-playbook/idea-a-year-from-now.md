Twelve months in, at a gym that kept it simple:

You've introduced dozens of members to a career they'd never seriously considered. Several have qualified. One or two coach on your floor. Others have built their own businesses and still train here.

Your staff spot potential without being asked. Members bring it up before you do. And your gym has quietly become known as a place that develops people, not just their training.

That last part is worth more than the commission. It's the thing that makes someone choose you over the cheaper gym down the road, and it isn't something you can buy or advertise. It only comes from having actually done it.

## The honest version

It won't feel like that in month three. It'll feel like you've put up posters and had a few conversations that went nowhere.

That's the normal shape of it. The members who enrol next spring are having their first thought about it right now, and nothing you can measure this week will tell you that's happening.

Keep the monthly rhythm. Judge yourself on conversations. The rest follows, or it doesn't, but it definitely doesn't if the conversations stop.

## The whole thing in one line

Every learner starts with one person asking:

```
Have you ever thought about doing this professionally?
```

Ask it often, and ask it genuinely.
