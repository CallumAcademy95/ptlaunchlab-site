A lot of people who end up qualifying started because a friend pushed them into it. This gives them the excuse.

## The week

- Guest passes for members to bring someone
- Academy posters where the guests will actually stand — reception, not the back corridor
- Coaches having the usual conversations, with guests included
- A countdown on social through the week

## The one question at reception

When a member signs a guest in:

```
Is your friend into fitness?
```

If yes, mention the academy. Naturally, once, then move on. You're talking to two people at that desk, and the guest has no history of ignoring your posters.

**Why it works:** deciding to change career alone is frightening. Deciding it alongside someone you train with is a plan. Two people enrolling together is more common than you'd guess, and they both finish more often than people who start alone.
