You earn £400 (inc. VAT) when a member enrols and pays in full, or £250 on the monthly plan. Whether you share any of it with your team is your choice. If you do, **£25 to the person who had the conversation** is a fair split: you keep the rest, it costs you nothing you didn't already gain, and it turns a good intention into something your team actually remembers on a Tuesday.

Pay it when your fee reaches you, not before. Your fee lands 30 days after a pay-in-full enrolment, or when the learner's 5th monthly payment clears.

## How to announce it

```
When someone enrols through the academy, whoever had the conversation with
them gets £25.

Not for selling. For noticing someone and mentioning it.
```

Keep it that simple. The moment it has tiers, splits and conditions, people stop bothering.

## Also reward the thing that leads to it

Enrolments are rare enough that a team could go two months with nothing, and enthusiasm dies in that gap. So recognise the activity too:

- Most academy conversations this month
- Best learner story shared
- Most QR referrals
- Coach spotter of the month

These don't need to cost anything. An hour off, a coffee, lunch, or being named in the team meeting. **Recognition beats cash more often than people expect**. The £25 is what makes it real, the recognition is what makes it repeat.

## Track it honestly

Whoever had the conversation gets it, even if someone else was on shift when the person enrolled. One argument about who gets the £25 will kill the whole scheme.
