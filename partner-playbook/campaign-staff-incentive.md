You earn £500 when a member enrols. **Pay £50 of it to the person who had the conversation.** You keep £450, it costs you nothing you didn't already gain, and it turns a good intention into something your team actually remembers on a Tuesday.

## How to announce it

```
When someone enrols through the academy, whoever had the conversation with
them gets £50.

Not for selling. For noticing someone and mentioning it.
```

Keep it that simple. The moment it has tiers, splits and conditions, people stop bothering.

## Also reward the thing that leads to it

Enrolments are rare enough that a team could go two months with nothing, and enthusiasm dies in that gap. So recognise the activity too:

- Most academy conversations this month
- Best learner story shared
- Most QR referrals
- Coach spotter of the month

These don't need to cost anything. An hour off, a coffee, lunch, or being named in the team meeting. **Recognition beats cash more often than people expect**. The £50 is what makes it real, the recognition is what makes it repeat.

## Track it honestly

Whoever had the conversation gets it, even if someone else was on shift when the person enrolled. One argument about who gets the £50 will kill the whole scheme.
