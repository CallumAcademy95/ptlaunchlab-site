---
title: The launch announcement
type: email
channel: Whole member list
when_to_use: Once, when you go live. Then again each January.
order: 10
---

The only email that goes to everybody. Every other one below is aimed at a smaller group and works harder for it.

```
Subject: Could fitness become your career?

Hi [First Name],

You've already invested in your health.

Have you ever thought about investing in your future?

[Gym Name] has partnered with PT Launch Lab to help our members become
qualified Personal Trainers.

Whether that's a full career change, a second income, or just learning
properly — we'd like to help you work out what's possible.

Reply to this, or grab one of the team next time you're in.

It starts with a conversation.

The [Gym Name] Team
```

Send it Tuesday or Wednesday morning. Not Monday — it competes with everything else people ignore on a Monday.
