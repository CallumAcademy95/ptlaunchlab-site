The first month is when your academy is newest, and new is the one thing that gets members asking on its own. Use it to get the word out properly.

There is no launch offer and no discount, now or later. The price is the same every day of the year: £999.99 in full, or 10 monthly payments of £99.99 with no interest. That is a strength, not a gap. Nobody has to wait for a better deal, and nobody who enrolled last week feels they paid too much.

Enrolment is rolling, so there is no deadline to push either. Members can start any day.

## Run it in two waves

**Weeks 1–2: say it exists**
Most of your members will not notice a new poster. Tell them, plainly, that your gym now runs its own Personal Training academy.

```
We've just opened our own Personal Training academy.

If becoming a PT has ever crossed your mind, you can now qualify
through the gym you already train in.

Ask at reception or scan the QR code.
```

**Weeks 3–4: answer the first objection**
For most people the barrier is time or money. Answer both.

```
Thinking about our PT academy but not sure you can fit it in?

It's 100% online, around your job, and you can pay monthly.

Ask at reception or scan the QR code.
```

## Where it goes

Your launch graphics are in your Resources. Put them:

- On the gym screen, in rotation
- On Instagram feed and Stories
- On your poster, at the front desk
- In one email to the whole member list, week one

## Four rules

**No made-up urgency.** No "limited time", no countdowns, no "last chance". There is no deadline, and members can tell when one is invented.

**Don't lead with the price in conversation.** Ask the question first, *"have you ever thought about doing this professionally?"*, and talk about the money once they're interested. Nobody changes career because of a price. They change career because the job appeals.

**Never invent a number.** Only quote the price above. If someone asks about a discount or a code, the honest answer is that there isn't one, for anyone.

**Tell your team it's live.** The most common failure is a member asking about the academy at the desk and being told nobody knows anything about it.

## What good looks like

One enrolment in the launch month is a good outcome: £250 to the gym and a member whose career changed. Two is a strong one.

Judge it on **conversations and enquiries**, not just enrolments. Somebody who asks during the launch and enrols in March still came from this campaign.
