This course includes two full qualifications, not one — and almost nobody at {{gymName}} realises it yet.

**The line:** *Level 2 and Level 3. One course, one price.*

## The month

| Week | Do this |
|---|---|
| **1** | Post the graphic and the caption. Both lead on the two qualifications, not the price |
| **2** | Story poll: *Did you know your PT course here is actually two qualifications?* Yes / No |
| **3** | Send the member email. Ask for a reply, not a click |
| **4** | Staff pick up everyone who answered the poll or replied |

The poll is the engine, same as January. Everyone who taps either answer has just been told something new about their own gym and is a named person your team can talk to on the floor.

## The caption

```
Here's something most of our members don't know about their own gym's academy.

This PT course is actually two qualifications — Level 2 and Level 3 — in one course, at one price.

If that's news to you, you're not the only one.
```

## The member email

```
Subject: A question about the PT course

Quick one.

This course covers both the NCFE Level 2 Certificate in Gym Instructing and the Level 3 Certificate in Personal Training — one course, one price.

There's a tutor from day one, and a £500 business mentorship community included, which is the part people are usually most surprised by.

If you've ever thought about it, reply to this and tell me where you're at. I'll tell you honestly whether it's worth your time.
```

Reply, not click. People who reply are in a conversation; people who click are on a page.

## WhatsApp / SMS

```
Did you know our PT course covers Level 2 AND Level 3? Reply if you want the detail.
```

## When someone replies

Do not pitch. Fill three baskets first — where they are now, what they actually want, and what stopped them last time. Only then say what the course is. The full framework is in **Conversation framework**.

## What to judge it on

Replies and conversations. **Not enrolments.** October conversations become January enrolments, and January is the biggest month of your year.
