Drop photographs of each gym in here and re-run:

  node --use-system-ca scripts/gym-tv-slides.mjs <slug>

JPG or PNG, landscape, ideally 1920x1080 or larger. They become slide
backgrounds, darkened from the left so the type stays readable and the gym
stays recognisable on the right.

What works: wide shots of the floor mid-session, a coach with a member,
reception, the free weights area. Busy beats empty.

What does not: posed stock imagery, anything with text already on it, or
photos of a different gym. The whole claim is "right here, in this gym".
