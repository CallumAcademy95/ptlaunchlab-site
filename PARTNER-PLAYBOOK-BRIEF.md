# Partner Playbook — content brief

**What this is:** the list of everything the playbook needs, so the data dump maps straight onto entries we can build. Fill in the **INPUT NEEDED** blocks and we turn them into `partner-playbook/*.md`.

**Where it lives:** `/partner-playbook/*.md` in this repo, rendered at `/partners/playbook`. Core entries are committed markdown — they're curated and we rewrite them constantly. Ad-hoc additions come through the admin upload (see §6).

---

## 0. The thing everyone gets wrong first

The playbook has **two audiences**, and copy written for one is useless to the other:

| | Who's talking | Who's listening | What lands |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Most of it** | The gym | Their own members | "You could do this for a living" |
| **The rest** | The gym owner | Their own staff | "Here's how to mention it, here's what you earn" |

This matters because our *ads* talk to gym owners about revenue — "add a revenue stream, zero cost". **None of that language belongs in the playbook.** A member doesn't care that the gym earns £500. They care that the thing they already do five times a week could pay their rent.

Every entry below is tagged with which audience it's for.

## 1. Social posts — `type: social`

The single most-requested item. A gym owner wants to open the playbook, copy a caption, post it, and move on.

**Format for each:** caption (ready to paste, with `[GYM NAME]` placeholders), suggested image or what to shoot, and the CTA. Platform noted because a LinkedIn-length caption dies on Instagram.

| # | Working title | Audience | Angle | Platform |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | We're now a PT academy | Members | Announcement. The one that goes out first. | IG + FB feed |
| S2 | Some of you already train everyone else | Members | The soft prompt — the member who's always spotting, always advising | IG feed |
| S3 | From member to trainer | Members | Proof. A real person who did it. **Strongest post we can have.** | IG feed / Reel |
| S4 | You don't need to be the fittest person here | Members | Objection: "I'm not in good enough shape" | IG feed |
| S5 | You're not too old | Members | Objection: age. Our biggest real audience is 30–45 career-changers | IG + FB |
| S6 | 8 weeks, around your job | Members | Objection: time. Online, flexible, keep working | IG story + feed |
| S7 | What it actually costs | Members | Price + payment plan, stated plainly. Removes the awkward question | IG story |
| S8 | Intake closes [DATE] | Members | Deadline. Reusable every intake | IG story |
| S9 | Ask me anything about becoming a PT | Members | Story question-sticker. Generates the DMs that convert | IG story |
| S10 | Meet [TRAINER] — they qualified with us | Members | Staff spotlight, doubles as proof | IG feed |

**INPUT NEEDED:**
- **At least one real member→PT story per gym** (name, what they did before, how long, where they are now, photo). S3 is the highest-converting post in the set and it can't be written without a real person.
- Current price and payment plan wording you're happy being public.
- Course length and format in one sentence.
- Anything you've posted before that worked — even roughly.

## 2. Member emails — `type: email`

Gyms have a member list and almost never use it for this.

| # | Working title | Audience | When it goes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Announcement to the whole list | Members | Launch, then once per intake | The workhorse |
| E2 | You know this place better than most | Lapsed members | Anytime | Lapsed members are a *better* audience than active ones — they liked the gym, lost the habit, and "career" beats "come back and train" |
| E3 | You've been asking good questions | Engaged members | Anytime | To people who ask about programming and nutrition. Highest intent in the building |
| E4 | Your PT wants to qualify | Freelance PTs / staff | Anytime | For unqualified or partly-qualified people already working the floor |
| E5 | Intake closes [DATE] | Anyone who enquired | 5–7 days before | Deadline |
| E6 | You asked about the academy | Enquirers | Within 24h | Follow-up after a DM or front-desk conversation |

**INPUT NEEDED:**
- What a gym typically emails members about, so tone matches (do they send newsletters? offers only?).
- Whether gyms send from their own platform or want us to supply plain text they paste.
- The enquiry-to-enrolment path today: DM, front desk, or straight to the academy link?

## 3. In-gym scripts — `type: script`

Spoken, not written. Short enough to remember. This is what the new partner meant by *"tips for how my staff should sell the course"*.

| # | Working title | Audience | Situation |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | "What's this poster?" | Staff → member | Front desk, 20 seconds, ends with the QR |
| C2 | The gym floor mention | PT → member | A PT talking to someone who's clearly interested in training properly |
| C3 | The tour close | Staff → prospect | New member joining who mentions fitness as a career |
| C4 | "How much is it?" | Staff → member | Say the number, then the payment plan. Never dodge it |
| C5 | "I haven't got time" | Staff → member | The single most common objection |
| C6 | "Will I actually get work?" | Staff → member | The real fear. Gym interviews, one-partner-per-area, the floor-rent path |
| C7 | What not to say | Owner → staff | **Important.** No guaranteeing income, no advising on finance, no "you'll definitely get a job here." Protects the gym and us |

**INPUT NEEDED:**
- What your own team says that works — you've had these conversations hundreds of times and that's the fastest way to get C1–C6 right.
- Anything a partner has said that you'd want corrected. C7 is written from real mistakes, not theory.

## 4. Campaign plays — `type: campaign`

Multi-step things a gym runs over days or weeks, not a single post.

| # | Working title | Audience | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | January intake | Members | The big one. Every gym is already talking about change in January |
| P2 | QR poster blitz | Owner | Where to put them and why. Front desk, changing room mirror, water fountain, the wall people face on the leg press |
| P3 | Staff incentive scheme | Owner → staff | **£50 per enrolment to the staff member, £450 to the gym.** How to announce it and track it |
| P4 | Member referral | Members | A member who recommends a friend |
| P5 | Screen advert rotation | Owner | What to run on the gym TV and how often to change it |
| P6 | Open evening | Owner | An hour in the gym, someone from PTLL on the call or in person |
| P7 | Class instructor → PT | Members | Class instructors are the warmest audience in any gym and almost always overlooked |

**INPUT NEEDED:**
- P3: confirm £50/£450 is what you want published.
- P6: are you willing to do these, and in person or on a call? Changes what we promise.
- Which of these you've already seen work at Ebor, MOF or Superflex.

## 5. Owner ideas — `type: idea` *(new category — needs a small code change)*

Not copy. This is the "how do I think about this" layer — what the new partner was reaching for with *"do you have any tips"*. Short, opinionated, one screen each.

| # | Working title | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | What good looks like | Realistic numbers. A gym of N members should expect roughly X enquiries and Y enrolments a year. **Sets expectations before they get disappointed** |
| O2 | Where enrolments actually come from | Honest ranking of the channels — conversation beats posters beats social |
| O3 | Your area is yours | One partner per area. What that's worth and why it's worth protecting |
| O4 | What to do with a qualified learner | Hire them, rent them floor space, or let them go. The commercial case for each |
| O5 | Don't sell it. Mention it | The tone that works. Gym members hate being sold to by their gym |

**INPUT NEEDED — this is where your experience is worth most:**
- **O1 is the important one and only you can write it.** Rough conversion reality: of the gyms running this, how many enrolments per year, off what membership size? Even ballpark. A partner with a wrong number in their head quits early.
- O4: what have partners actually done with graduates?

## 6. Adding entries without a deploy

Core entries stay as committed markdown — they're curated, reviewed and rewritten often, and a database turns every copy tweak into a data migration.

But a one-off — a seasonal post, a PDF someone wants shared this week — shouldn't need a deploy. So the playbook reads **both**:

1. `partner-playbook/*.md` in the repo — the curated core
2. Entries added through `/admin/partners` — ad-hoc, stored in the database, optionally with a file attached

Both render identically. Repo entries win on a slug clash, so the curated version is never silently overridden by an upload.
