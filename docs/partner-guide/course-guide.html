The 60-second version

What it is

The NCFE Level 3 Diploma in Gym Instructing and Personal Training — 12 units, studied online on Praxel, with practical sessions filmed in a gym.

Who does what

PT Launch Lab teaches, marks and awards. Your gym refers members and hosts the practical. Nobody at the gym marks anything.

The one thing to remember

If a learner asks you a course question you are not sure about, send them to their tutor rather than guessing. Wrong answers cost them a resubmission.

How Praxel works

The learning platform

Praxel is where the course actually happens — not a portal that links out somewhere else.

One sign-in

Learners sign in at ptll.praxel.co.uk with their own account.

Everything in one place

Units, workbook tasks, assessor feedback and their evidence all live on the same site.

Works on a phone

No laptop needed — learners can read, answer and submit from their phone.

  • Units are worked in order — a learner can't skip ahead to unit 12.
  • Every task saves itself as they type; nothing is lost if they close the tab.
  • Once a unit is submitted, it sits with their assessor — there's nothing more for the learner to do until feedback lands.
  • Feedback can send a unit back for another go. That's normal marking, not a sign anything has gone wrong.
