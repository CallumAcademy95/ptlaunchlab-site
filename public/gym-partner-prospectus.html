The problem this solves

Most independent gyms lose good trainers to other clubs, struggle to replace them quickly, and carry empty floor space while they look. Hiring is reactive: you only start when someone leaves, and you are dependent on the right person happening to be on the market that month.

Meanwhile you already have members who would like to work in the industry and have no idea how to start. Most of them go elsewhere to qualify, and then work elsewhere too.

How it works

1. Your branded academy

Your logo, your own enrolment link and a QR code for the gym. Members scan, enrol and qualify under your brand.

2. We deliver all of it

Tutoring, assessment, certification and learner support. NCFE Level 2 and Level 3, Ofqual regulated, CIMSPA recognised. Most learners qualify in 8 to 16 weeks around a job. You teach nothing.

3. You are paid

£400 per learner who pays in full (£250 monthly), rising to £500 / £300 from your 4th learner in a quarter, all inc. VAT (the Partner Accelerator rate, no cap). Paid 30 days after a pay-in-full enrolment, or when their 5th monthly payment clears. Nothing is clawed back once paid.

4. First introductions

Learners who qualify through your academy are introduced to you first. A trainer who qualified in your gym may well choose to rent space from you.

What it costs you: nothing. No fee, no minimum, no tie-in, and either side can end it on 30 days' written notice. Each month you get a marketing kit (screen video, posters, social posts) and a ready-to-run Meta ad pack.

The commercial terms

Reproduced from the partnership agreement v4.2. These are the terms you would sign, not a summary written for a brochure.

Clause 5

Commission schedule, inclusive of VAT

You are paid £400 for each learner who pays in full and £250 for each learner on the monthly plan. From the 4th learner who enrols through your gym in a calendar quarter, that learner and each later one that quarter earns £500 (pay in full) or £300 (monthly) — the Partner Accelerator rate. Your first 3 learners in the quarter stay at £400 / £250. If you choose to give your members a saving on the pay-in-full price, it comes out of your fee. All figures include VAT — nothing is added on top.

Clause 5.4

When the money is released

If the learner pays in full, commission is paid 30 days after enrolment. If they pay monthly, it is paid once their 5th monthly payment has been collected. If a learner is refunded or stops paying before that point, no commission is due on them. You can see accrued and released commission in the partner portal at any time.

Clauses 5.7–5.9

No clawback once paid

Once commission has been paid to you it is yours. It is only repayable if the enrolment turns out to be fraudulent or invalid.

Clause 14

Learner non-solicitation

During the partnership and for 6 months after it ends, you agree not to sell competing PT education or certification to learners we introduced. This does not stop you employing them, renting them floor space, or running your own gym however you like.

On territory

One gym per area is written into the agreement: while you are a partner we will not sign another partner gym within 5 miles of your premises (clause 3.8). We will tell you honestly whether anyone near you is already set up before you sign.
