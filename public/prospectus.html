How it works

Four steps, from your current job to a gym floor.

01
Learn

Study online, at your own pace

The course runs on Praxel, our learning platform. You study when it suits you, around work and family. Your personal tutor is assigned within 24 hours of enrolling and is there for your questions all the way through.

02
Qualify

Practicals filmed at any gym

The practical assessments are video-assessed. You film yourself coaching at any gym, and a tutor reviews it. There's no in-person training and nothing to travel to. You finish with the NCFE Level 2 & 3.

03

A guaranteed warm introduction

Once you qualify, we approach at least one gym on your behalf: one in our partner network, or one local to you. It's a warm introduction, not a job-board listing. Whether they interview you, and whether you get the job, is their call. We've hired 500+ PTs ourselves, so we know what gyms look for and we prepare you for it.

04
Build

The Mentorship Hub

Included in the fee. A private dashboard built to walk you from qualifying to your first paying client, with Callum, Miles and Ryan a message away when you're stuck.

What you'll study

One qualification. Twelve units.

You study for the NCFE Level 3 Diploma in Gym Instructing and Personal Training (Ofqual ref 603/4388/6). Units 1 to 6 cover gym instructing at Level 2. Units 7 to 12 take you to Level 3 personal training.

Level 2 · Gym instructing
1Anatomy and Physiology for ExerciseT/617/4001
2Maximising the Customer Experience in a Gym EnvironmentA/617/4002
3Supporting Client Health and Well-beingD/617/4008
4Conducting Client Consultations and Gym InductionsF/617/4003
5Planning and Reviewing Gym-Based Exercise ProgrammesR/617/4006
6Instructing and Supervising Gym-Based Exercise ProgrammesY/617/4007
Level 3 · Personal training
7Applied Anatomy and Physiology for Activity, Health and FitnessD/617/1707
8Client Motivation and Lifestyle ManagementH/617/1708
9Programming Personal Training SessionsK/617/1709
10Delivering Personal Training SessionsD/617/1710
11Nutrition to Support a Physical Activity ProgrammeH/617/1711
12Business Acumen for Personal TrainersK/617/1712

Every unit is studied online on Praxel. The codes on the right are the unit reference numbers.
