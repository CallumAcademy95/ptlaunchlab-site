## What this means for the Phase 5 rewrite

**The ranking risk that was holding up the location rewrite is close to nil.**

- The whole domain ranks for **99 keywords**. **Zero in the top 3, two in the
  top 10.** Combined estimated traffic value is 42.
- **84 of the 99 land on the homepage**, and most are other companies' brand
  names the homepage picks up by accident: *pt lab*, *pt ressa*, *tdf the lab*,
  *training lab*, *neon pt*, *platform pt*, *the move lab newcastle under lyme*.
  Those are not terms a copy rewrite can lose.
- **The location system ranks for three keywords in total.** Best position 23.
- The "961 pages" figure overstates what is exposed. Only **one** of the 31
  `[location]` templates is canonical: the other 30 all declare
  `alternates.canonical` pointing at `level-3-personal-trainer-course/<slug>`,
  and `app/sitemap.ts` advertises only that one route. So Google is being told
  930 of those pages are duplicates, and the real surface is **31 canonical
  location URLs** ranking for three keywords between them.

The earlier plan treated the location pages as the highest-risk surface because
of the page count. On this evidence the page count was the wrong measure: there
is very little ranking equity there to protect, and the rewrite can proceed
without the caution that was blocking it.

**Two caveats.** This is DataForSEO's index at one moment, and it only counts
keywords in their tracked database, so genuinely tiny long-tail location terms
may be invisible here. And a page ranking for nothing is not proof it is
indexed. Worth a Search Console check before concluding the pages do nothing.
