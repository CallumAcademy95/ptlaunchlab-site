# Link outreach — ptlaunchlab.co.uk

**Why this exists.** The site has ~262k words across 79 pages and near-zero organic
visibility: 0 keywords in positions 1–3, best commercial term ("level 3 fitness
instructor courses", 1,600/mo) at **position 65**. Cause is not content. It is
**~6 dofollow referring main domains** against OriGym's ~1,650, FutureFit's ~1,180
and HFE's ~1,000. Pages that rank for our targets carry 30–120 referring domains.

**Goal: 40+ genuine dofollow referring domains.** That is the number that makes
existing pages competitive. Everything below is a relationship we already have.

**Rules that make these links count**

- **In-content, not footer.** 411 of our current 744 referring pages are nav/sitewide
  links and Google discounts them. Only 12 are in-article. Ask for a mention in the
  body of a page or post.
- **Deep links, not homepage.** Point each one at the page it is actually relevant
  to. Homepage links help far less.
- **Vary the anchor.** Not everyone using "personal trainer course" — mix brand
  ("PT Launch Lab"), natural phrases ("qualified with"), and topic anchors.
- **No payment, no reciprocal-link schemes.** Both are link schemes under Google's
  spam policy and we already carry a spam score of 27. This has to be clean.

## Message template — podcast guest

> Subject: your episode is live — plus a link for your site
>
> Hi [name],
>
> Your episode is live: [episode URL]
>
> It's had a good response — [specific detail].
>
> If you want to put it on your own site or share it, the link above is the one to
> use. Happy to send the audiogram and a couple of pull-quotes if useful.
>
> Thanks again for coming on.
>
> Callum

Do not mention SEO or ask for a "backlink". Give them something to link to and make
it easy. Most people link without being asked once they have the URL in hand.
